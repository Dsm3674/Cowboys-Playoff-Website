OUR STORY

Where the Idea Began

LoneStar Analytics started as a small experimental project during the 2024–2025 NFL season — a season full of momentum swings, quarterback surges, heartbreaking losses, and the constant question every Cowboys fan asks:

“What are the REAL chances we make the playoffs?”

That question sparked the idea: build a fully open-source, modern NFL analytics engine powered by machine learning, Monte Carlo simulations, and advanced player-impact modeling.

What Inspired Us

  • ⚙️ The lack of **transparent, open-source NFL prediction models**
  • 🤖 The rise of **AI-driven sports simulations** and probabilistic forecasting
  • 📊 Our own obsession with tracking the Cowboys’ playoff odds daily
  • 🏈 A belief that fans deserve **real analytics**, not “hot takes”

What began as a simple playoff-odds calculator evolved into a full analytics platform: real-time projections, player impact scoring, fan sentiment tracking, interactive simulations, and clean, modern data-visualizations — all built with accessibility and openness in mind.

Open Source for a Reason

We believe analytics should be accessible. That’s why the entire project — from the prediction engine to the UI — is **100% open-source** and free for the community to improve, fork, or build upon.
